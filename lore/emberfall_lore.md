# Emberfall — World Lore

## The World
The village of **Emberfall** lies in a narrow valley beneath the **Shadow Peaks**. A cold mountain river, the Ashwash, runs through it. Winters are long; the peaks trap storms for months. The village survives on goat herding, iron mining, and tolls from the only pass road north.

An old superstition: when the peak called **Cinder Crown** smokes at dawn, someone in the valley will die before the next thaw.

## Key Locations

### The Gilded Boar Tavern
The only tavern in Emberfall. Thick oak doors, sawdust floors, lamplight always burning. The back room has a locked cellar door that does not appear on any building plan.

### The Shrine of the Pale Flame
A small stone shrine at the village edge. A white flame burns there without fuel and has never gone out. The priestess **Sister Anselm** tends it and reads omens in its flickering.

### The Old Mine (The Hollow)
An abandoned iron mine east of the village. Miners broke into a carved stone passage twenty years ago and sealed it the same night. Three miners never came back. The company forbids entry, but lanterns are sometimes seen inside.

### Mira's Secret Passage
The tavern keeper **Mira Voss** knows a secret passage from the tavern cellar to the old shrine crypt. She uses it to smuggle medicine and letters during the winter tolls. Only she and Sister Anselm know the full route.

## Factions

### The Tollwardens
Mercenary guards who control the pass road in winter. Led by **Captain Draven Kess**. They charge steep tolls and seize "contraband" — including medicine. They want the mine reopened under their control.

### The Pale Order
Quiet monks serving the Pale Flame. They keep records of every death in the valley going back 200 years. They believe the flame dims whenever someone lies inside the shrine.

### The Hollow Crew
Former miners who drink at the Gilded Boar. They swear the sealed passage breathes cold air and that knocking answers from below. Their leader, **Old Tam**, carries the original mine key.

## Important NPCs

- **Mira Voss** — Tavern keeper, mid-40s, practical braid, scar on left hand. Knows the secret passage. Distrusts the Tollwardens after they seized her winter medicine shipment. Will help travelers who stand up to Draven.
- **Captain Draven Kess** — Tollwarden commander. Polite, patient, dangerous. Offers gold for information about smuggling. Never enters the shrine.
- **Sister Anselm** — Priestess of the Pale Flame. Elderly, half-blind, unfailingly honest. Can confirm whether someone lied in the shrine by watching the flame.
- **Old Tam** — Retired miner. Drunk, haunted, loyal. Holds the mine key and will give it to anyone who promises to find out what happened to his crew.

## Magic & Rules
- The **Pale Flame** reveals lies told within the shrine: it gutters and turns blue.
- Iron from the Hollow mine rings faintly when struck — locals call it "hollow iron." Weapons forged from it harm things that bleed shadow.
- The sealed passage cannot be opened by force alone; it needs the mine key **and** a prayer spoken at the Pale Flame.

## Adventure Hooks
1. Mira asks travelers to escort medicine through the secret passage while Tollwardens search the tavern.
2. Old Tam begs someone to take the mine key and learn what knocks back from below.
3. Sister Anselm reports the flame turned blue for the first time in a decade — someone lied in the shrine last night.
